# Right of Publicity Stance

_Last updated: 2026-05-08_

## Attribution Policy

Cited displays short, deep-linked clips from *The Diary of a CEO* (DOAC) podcast. Each clip is attributed to the **named credentialed guest** — the expert or speaker appearing in the episode.

- Clips are attributed by full name and credentials (e.g., "Dr. Rhonda Patrick, PhD — Biochemist").
- Claims are attributed to the guest who made them, not to the podcast host.
- Cited never implies endorsement — never implies that any guest, host, or podcast endorses the Cited application or any habit recommendation derived from their content.
- Cited never uses guest or host likenesses (photos, voice recordings) in marketing material outside the clip-card surface where the YouTube embed appears.

## Fair Use Posture

Cited's use of DOAC clips rests on a fair use analysis:

- **Factor 1 (transformative)**: Clips are used to operationalize specific health claims into actionable habits with original commentary and habit-tracking context. This is transformative — it is not a substitute for watching the original episode.
- **Factor 4 (market harm)**: Cited drives traffic back to DOAC via deep links. It does not compete with DOAC's market or provide a substitute for the podcast.
- **Factor 3 (amount)**: Clips are as brief as needed to convey the specific claim. See `MEDICAL_REVIEW.md` for length guidance.
- **Factor 2 (nature)**: DOAC episodes are published works; fair use applies more readily to published works.

## Takedown Process

Any named guest, host, or rights holder may request removal of any clip via:

1. Opening a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/trhoang220703/cited/issues) labeled `takedown-request`
2. Emailing **legal@cited.dev** (placeholder — Phase 4)

We respond within **48 hours** and remove or block the clip within the same window pending review.

Clips removed via takedown are hard-deleted from the database and cannot be re-added without express written permission from the rights holder.
