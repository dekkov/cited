<context>
Why we pivoted: user pushed back on LLM-generated onboarding questions. Per-turn streaming is slow, unreliable (hung on "Getting your first question..."), expensive, and unnecessary — the AI's real value is at SYNTHESIS time, not in generating "what's your sleep like?"

The new flow front-loads ALL the personalization signal (free-form "tell us about yourself" dump), uses a cheap LLM call to rank a curated question pool by relevance, then lets the user answer chips quickly. The reasoning LLM only runs once, on the full picture, at the end.

Plan 03-04's original 19-step visual checklist still has parts that apply (no red colors, no flame emoji, palette, returning-user redirect, settings re-run button — though settings re-run needs to be verified against the new flow), but the streaming/per-turn/synthesis-loader/tell-me-more steps are gone.
</context>
